ABOUT

Cognition is the quest.
Computation is the lens.

I am Ivilin Peev Stoianov, a senior researcher at the Institute of Cognitive Sciences and Technologies of the National Research Council of Italy (CNR–ISTC), based in Padova.

I develop computational accounts of human cognition, bringing models into dialogue with behavioral experiments and neural data. Predictive coding and active inference are central to this work.

I also explore how these ideas can inform robot control and human–robot interaction.
